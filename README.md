# 🍀 Alex “The Wizard” of the Spaghetti Dungeon 🍝

A comedic fantasy tavern song about a pasta-wielding wizard named Alex, enchanted noodles, bouncing meatballs, ravioli dragons, and a feast that lasts all night.

## About the Song

**“Alex ‘The Wizard’ of the Spaghetti Dungeon”** is an upbeat Celtic-inspired fantasy song written in the style of a rowdy tavern singalong.

The song follows Alex, a powerful and slightly chaotic wizard who travels through the mysterious Spaghetti Dungeon. Along the way, Alex encounters noodle caves, meat-sauce rivers, ravioli dragons, an overconfident Goblin Chef, musical meatballs, and an endless supply of pasta.

The concept combines:

- Celtic tavern music
- Fantasy role-playing adventures
- Comedic storytelling
- Italian food
- Group singalong choruses
- An unreasonable amount of spaghetti

## Creative Direction

The song was designed to sound like something performed in a crowded fantasy tavern.

The verses tell the story of Alex’s journey through the Spaghetti Dungeon, while the chorus is written as a simple call-and-response section that listeners can sing together.

The arrangement can gradually become larger and more chaotic as the song continues:

1. Begin with a single acoustic instrument or tavern ambience.
2. Introduce fiddle, guitar, and percussion during the first verse.
3. Add group vocals during the first chorus.
4. Increase the instrumental energy during the second verse.
5. Make the bridge feel like a full tavern celebration.
6. Finish with a large ensemble, shouted vocals, clinking mugs, and a final sustained chord.

## Lyrics

### Verse 1

Oh gather ’round ye lads and lasses, pull your chairs up near,  
I’ve got a tale of wizardry you’ll surely want to hear.  
Of Alex with his magic staff and beard of golden flame,  
The Spaghetti Dungeon fears the mighty sound of his name!

With a meatball in one pocket and garlic in his hand,  
He wandered through the noodle caves across the Pasta Land.  
Where ravioli dragons roared and meat sauce rivers flowed,  
He laughed and cried, “Another bowl!” and down the tunnel strode.

### Chorus

Hey! Ho! Raise your forks up high!  
Dance beneath the noodle sky!  
Spin the pasta, drink the ale,  
Wizard Alex tells the tale!

Hey! Ho! Give the pot a stir!  
Nobody casts spells like her... him!  
Through the Dungeon, bold and bright—  
Alex keeps the noodles right!

### Verse 2

The Goblin Chef came stomping in with parmesan so old,  
He claimed his secret spaghetti sauce was worth its weight in gold.  
Alex winked, twirled his wand around, and with a flash of light,  
Turned every noodle perfectly al dente overnight.

The meatballs started bouncing to the fiddler’s merry tune,  
While ravioli floated like balloons beneath the moon.  
The garlic bread applauded with a buttery delight,  
Even grumpy trolls were singing through the middle of the night.

### Bridge

Fiddles started flying!  
Tin whistles filled the air!  
Every bowl was bottomless,  
There was pasta everywhere!

Someone yelled, “More meatballs!”  
Someone cried, “More cheese!”  
Alex simply waved his wand—  
“Both of those? Yes, please!”

### Final Chorus

Hey! Ho! Raise your forks once more!  
Magic spills across the floor!  
Ale is flowing, spirits soar,  
Everyone is asking for more!

So if your travels lead someday  
Where enchanted noodles play,  
Listen for the joyful tune,  
Echoing beneath the moon.

You’ll hear laughter, clinking mugs,  
Songs that every tavern knows.  
For Alex, Wizard of the Dungeon,  
Where the finest pasta grows!

One more round! One more song!  
The Wizard’s feast goes all night long!  
With every bowl and every bite—  
The Spaghetti Dungeon shines tonight!
